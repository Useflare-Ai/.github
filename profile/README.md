## What FLARE does

**Creator discovery**
Search around 950,000 TikTok creators by niche, audience size, region and real sponsorship history, then shortlist the ones that fit.

**Sponsorship intelligence**
See which brands a creator has worked with, how often and how recently, with estimates clearly labelled as estimates.

**Brand research**
Open any brand to see the creators it works with, its activity over time and the categories it invests in.

**Agency intelligence**
Browse talent agencies and the creators linked to them, with the source of each link shown.

**Creator and brand matching**
Find creators who fit a brand, based on the categories and audiences that brand already works with.

**YouTube creators**
Discover YouTube channels with 100,000+ subscribers alongside TikTok.

**Outreach for agencies**
Build brand shortlists and manage outreach and replies in one workspace, with suppression and opt-out handling built in.

## How we build

**Evidence first.** Every partnership, estimate and relationship shows where it comes from. Estimates are labelled as estimates.

**Fresh data, clearly dated.** Creator profiles carry the date they were last checked.

**Privacy by design.** Customer conversations, contact details and account data stay private.
